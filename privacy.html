Privacy Policy for Banana Log

Effective Date: July 16, 2026

Snow Anteater LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Banana Log mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

Banana Log is a local-first application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal fitness logs, workout data, or personal details to external servers. All data you input remains strictly on your local device.

2. Permissions Required

The App may request local device storage permissions to backup and export your workout data. These files are stored locally on your device and are never shared with us.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs that harvest or monitor your data.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
