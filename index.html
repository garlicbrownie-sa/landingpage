About Us

Snow Anteater LLC is an independent software development studio focused on crafting minimal, highly functional, and privacy-first mobile applications. We believe in local-first data architecture, clean user interfaces, and building tools that respect our users.

Our Apps

Banana Log

Banana Log is a clean, privacy-focused workout tracker built to strip away the digital noise and get straight to what matters: your progress.

While we originally designed it with calisthenics athletes in mind—perfect for tracking planks, pull-ups, and bodyweight progressions—we quickly realized its clean simplicity makes it a powerhouse for traditional weightlifters, too. Whether you are mastering your own bodyweight, moving heavy barbells, or mixing the absolute best of both worlds, we invite you to give it a try.

No accounts, no ads, no social feeds, and no tracking pixels. Just your training, stored safely and strictly on your own device.
